About

Himanshu is a software engineer and open-source contributor studying at IIT Guwahati. He started out building mobile apps in Flutter, then moved into full-stack and AI-driven systems — most recently pairing language models with live data feeds to build tools that hold up under real time pressure.

He's a regular on the hackathon and open-source circuit: a Top 1% finish at MLH's Global Hack Week, Super Contributor status at Hacktoberfest 2025, and builds ranging from a Jira race-engineering agent to a scholarship-matching platform used by students. He's currently teaching himself machine learning from Aurélien Géron and François Chollet's texts.

Off the keyboard, he follows Formula 1 closely — which is where this page's timing-tower styling comes from.
